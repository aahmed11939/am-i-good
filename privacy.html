Am I Good? App - Privacy Policy Effective Date: February 9, 2026 1. Introduction Am I Good? (“the App”) is a private self reflection and habit tracking application designed to help users log and reflect on personal actions they define for themselves. Your privacy is important, and the App is built to operate without collecting or transmitting personal data. ________________________________________ 2. Information We Collect We do not collect any personal data. Specifically: • The App does not collect names, email addresses, or identifiers. • The App does not collect usage data or analytics. • The App does not collect device information. • The App does not track users across apps or websites. All information entered into the App is created and controlled entirely by the user. ________________________________________ 3. Data Storage All data entered into Am I Good? is stored locally on the user’s device only. • No data is transmitted to external servers. • No cloud storage or synchronization is used. • No backups are created outside the device by the App. If the App is deleted, all associated data is permanently removed from the device. ________________________________________ 4. Accounts and Authentication The App may use a local, device only credential (such as an email/password combination) solely to protect access to the App on the user’s device. • This information is stored locally on the device. • No authentication data is transmitted or stored externally. • No account recovery or remote access is provided. ________________________________________ 5. Third Party Services The App does not use: • Third party analytics • Advertising networks • Social media integrations • External APIs or SDKs ________________________________________ 6. Data Sharing We do not share data with third parties. The App includes: • No social features • No comparisons with other users • No public or private sharing of data ________________________________________ 7. Children’s Privacy Am I Good? does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Since no data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal information stored about users of any age. ________________________________________ 8. Changes to This Policy If the App’s functionality changes in a way that affects privacy, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly. Any changes will be reflected in the publicly available version of this policy. ________________________________________ 9.
